Rules & Guide

📌 How it works

  1. Fill in your details on the Book Token page.
  2. Get your token number and QR slip.
  3. Watch the Live Queue and wait for your turn.

⚡ Rules

  • Senior citizens get priority entry.
  • Keep your original ID ready at Counter 4.
  • Missed tokens need re-registration.

❓ FAQ

What does VIP priority mean?

Senior citizens and urgent cases are moved ahead of normal tokens.

What if I miss my token?

Your token expires. Register again and you will get a new number.

How long will I wait?

On average 4 minutes per visitor. The dashboard shows the estimate.
